I compared several online cuneiform diagrams and transliteration guides and chose a reference book in a modern alphabetic style that provided the clearest one-to-one correspondence between Latin letters and cuneiform characters. Although this approach does not reproduce historical writing methods with complete accuracy, it has allowed me to reinterpret my own text using the visual language of Cuneiform and explore how modern information can be encoded using one of the oldest known writing systems.
